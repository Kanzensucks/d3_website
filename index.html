How Bad Is Road Trauma In Australia? Who Does It Most Affect?

Road deaths make headlines, but for every fatality many more people survive and end up in hospital — over 400,000 Australians between 2011 and 2021. This site explores a decade of hospitalised road-crash injuries: how the burden has grown, where it falls hardest, and who is most at risk.

The central finding

One territory drives on a different road. Adjusted for population, the Northern Territory's hospitalisation rate sits above the national average every single year of the decade — climbing to more than double the average in recent years.

Most of Australia sits in a narrow band around the average; the NT never does. See it for yourself on the interactive map.
