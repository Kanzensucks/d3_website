📌 Key Finding

The Northern Territory records rates 1.8–3× the national average every year — a persistent regional inequality.

About this project
This visualisation was developed as part of COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology, Semester 1 2026. The project explores 10 years of hospitalised road crash injury data published by BITRE.

Built with plain D3.js v7 (CDN), vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No framework, no bundler. Data processed with KNIME Analytics Platform.
